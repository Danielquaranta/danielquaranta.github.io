Daniel Quaranta

Composer · Researcher

Daniel Quaranta is Full Professor at the Federal University of the State of Rio de Janeiro (UNIRIO), in the Department of Composition and Conducting and in the Graduate Program in Music, which he has coordinated; he has also served as interim director of the Villa-Lobos Institute.

He holds a CNPq Research Productivity Fellowship (since 2026) and is part of FAPERJ’s “Cientista do Nosso Estado” program (since 2022). He leads the research group Práticas Sonoras Contemporâneas de Borda (Contemporary Sound Practices from the Edge, CNPq).

As a composer, he works in contemporary electroacoustic and instrumental music. He received the Reina Sofía Prize (Spain) in 2004 and 2009 and was awarded by the Ibermúsicas program in 2017 and 2019. His works have been published in Germany, Brazil and Mexico and performed in Latin America, Europe, North America and Asia.

He was a postdoctoral researcher at the Centro Mexicano para la Música y las Artes Sonoras (CMMAS, 2014–2015) and a visiting professor at UNAM (2023). He has held artist residencies at CMMAS, at Dias de Música Eletroacústica (Portugal), at the ICST of the Zurich University of the Arts (2018) and at the Métamorphoses d’Orphée studios (Belgium, 2019).

Born in Buenos Aires, he has lived in Brazil since 1995. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Composition and a doctorate in Music from UNIRIO, and a master’s degree in Music from UFRJ.

Research

Práticas Sonoras Contemporâneas de Borda

The research group began in 2016 as an archive of interviews, analyses and bibliography on Latin American composers, with the aim of bringing this repertoire into the curricula of our universities.

In 2020, during the pandemic, the archive became a collective practice: the Seminários Dispersos em Criação Musical (Dispersed Seminars in Musical Creation), horizontal meetings in which a composer presents their work and talks with students, teachers and researchers. There is no keynote lecture; there is conversation.

Since then the seminars have hosted guests from Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Chile, Spain, Portugal and France. The first volume of conversations was published by Mauad in 2025.

Network

The research has grown into a collaborative network with the Universidad Nacional de La Plata (Ibermúsicas exchange, 2022–2025), the Universidad Nacional de las Artes, CMMAS/UNAM, UNESPAR, the Universidad de las Artes de Guayaquil and the Federal University of Mato Grosso.

Support: FAPERJ – Cientista do Nosso Estado (2022 and 2025), FAPERJ – Publishing (2024), CNPq – Research Productivity, Ibermúsicas and CAPES.

Members and collaborators: Pedro Leal David, Rodolfo Pereira dos Santos, Carolina Carrizo (UNLP), Ricardo Vieira, Isabelle Ferreira, Lucas Cassano, Martín Robbio.
